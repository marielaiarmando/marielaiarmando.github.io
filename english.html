About Me

Hi! I'm Mariela Armando, and I love creating stories that captivate readers from the very first page.

I write psychological thrillers, suspense novels, and short stories. Some of my stories also explore romance, but they all share mystery, tension, and characters who must face decisions capable of changing everything.

If you enjoy stories that keep you intrigued until the very last page, I hope you find your next unforgettable read among my books.

My wish is that every story makes you feel, surprises you, and stays with you long after you finish reading.

El Lado Oscuro

Thriller · Suspense · Romance

What would you do if, overnight, your life changed completely and your only goal became survival?

There are very few things I know for certain. One of them is that Michael came into my life to help me. Everything else... I question.

I don't know who is watching me or why.
I can't tell dreams from reality anymore.
And I'm not sure Alex is who he claims to be.

I only know that I need to keep a clear mind and not let my impulses take control. Because if my suspicions are true, it may already be too late.
